0:00 - 0:30 • The Persona & Rural Reality Hook

"Respected judges, meet Sunita Devi. She is an ASHA community health worker in Sub-Centre Pipraich, Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh, responsible for 1,048 villagers across 182 households. Every single day, Sunita walks 6 to 8 kilometers visiting pregnant mothers, newborns, and teenage girls. She manually writes everything into five thick paper registers. In rural UP, connectivity is patchy or dead. If she misses a vital danger sign—like severe pre-eclampsia or severe anemia—a young mother can die of preventable complications before reaching the hospital. At month-end, she spends 4 exhausting days reconciling hand tallies for official government Form 6 paperwork. We built ASHA Saheli to change that forever."

0:30 - 1:10 • Voice Visit Logging Core Feature 1

"Here is how Sunita uses it in the field. Standing in a village courtyard, without typing a single letter, Sunita simply taps the mic and speaks naturally in local Hindi: 'रेखा, 7 महीने, बीपी 150/100, पैरों में सूजन' ('Rekha, 7 months, BP 150/100, swelling in feet'). Our dual-engine architecture activates: with zero internet connection, our on-device regex NLP extractor parses the name, gestational age, systolic/diastolic blood pressure, and danger symptoms with 0ms latency. When connectivity is present, Gemini 3.8 Flash handles rich unstructured dialects. The entire visit card is structured in under 2 seconds."

1:10 - 1:45 • Transparent Clinical Risk Flagging Core Feature 2

"Notice what immediately happens on screen: A prominent RED ALERT BANNER flashes. We do not use a black-box AI that hallucinates medical advice. Our clinical triage engine is 100% transparent, built on strict National Health Mission and WHO maternal-child danger protocols. Because Rekha's blood pressure is 150/100 with edema, the app flags impending eclampsia. The app speaks aloud in Hindi so Sunita can listen hands-free: 'रेखा: उच्च जोखिम। बीपी 150/100 अत्यधिक अधिक है। आज ही PHC रेफर करें।' It gives one-tap shortcuts to dial 108 Ambulance and alert ANM Rita Maurya immediately."

1:45 - 2:15 • 100% Offline-First Architecture Architecture

"Judges, rural health technology fails if it requires Wi-Fi or 4G. ASHA Saheli is built offline-first. Even in airplane mode or the deepest village gullies, all records, clinical danger algorithms, and due lists operate completely locally. When Sunita walks back toward the Sub-Centre and gets network, the pending sync queue automatically and securely synchronizes with the district RCH portal without losing a single data point."

2:15 - 2:40 • Auto-Generated Paperwork & Form 6 Core Feature 3

"Instead of wasting 4 days hand-counting tick marks, Sunita taps the Reports tab. The app has already tabulated her monthly MoHFW Form 6: registered pregnant women, high-risk cases tracked, and institutional deliveries. It even calculates her government performance-based incentive—₹3,150—ready for PFMS approval. In one click, she downloads the official vector PDF report and exports an Excel register."

2:40 - 3:00 • Adolescent Module & Impact RKSK & Closing

"Finally, under the RKSK Adolescent module, Sunita tracks weekly blue iron tablets and menstrual hygiene with a PIN-protected Privacy Shield during open village meetings. ASHA Saheli transforms the ASHA worker from an overwhelmed paper clerk into a digitally supercharged frontline clinician. We save lives by catching danger signs early, and we save hours of paperwork every single week. Thank you."

🎯 Judge Q&A Cheat Sheet

Q: "Why didn't you let an LLM decide the medical risk level?"

A: "Because clinical hallucinations in maternal health can be fatal. LLMs are best at language extraction and transcription, but medical triage must be deterministic, explainable, and certified by MoHFW guidelines. Our rule engine is transparent and verifiable."

Q: "How does this work when there's zero internet in remote hamlets?"

A: "Everything is stored in browser IndexedDB/LocalStorage. The offline regex engine extracts Hindi terms locally. The app runs completely in airplane mode and synchronizes when network returns."

Q: "What incentives do ASHAs have to adopt this?"

A: "Two huge incentives: 1) It eliminates 4 days of grueling register tallying at month end, and 2) It auto-calculates their government performance-based payment claim (₹3,150+) ensuring they get paid accurately without missed credits."
